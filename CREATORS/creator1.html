ABOUT THE CREATOR

Eiichiro Oda

Eiichiro Oda is a Japanese manga artist best known for creating One Piece. His work is recognized for its expansive world-building, memorable characters, humor, adventure, and long-form storytelling.

One Piece began serialization in 1997 and developed into a major global franchise spanning manga, anime, films, games, merchandise, and other adaptations.
