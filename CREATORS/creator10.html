ABOUT THE CREATOR

Boichi

Boichi is a South Korean-born manga artist who has worked extensively in Japan. He is recognized for his highly detailed artwork, dynamic characters, and strong use of realistic visual techniques.

His international recognition grew significantly through his artwork for Dr. STONE, written by Riichiro Inagaki.
