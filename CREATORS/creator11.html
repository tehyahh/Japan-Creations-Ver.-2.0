ABOUT THE CREATOR

Makoto Shinkai

Makoto Shinkai is a Japanese filmmaker, animator, writer, and director whose work is recognized for atmospheric visuals, emotional storytelling, detailed backgrounds, and themes involving human connection and separation.

He gained worldwide recognition with Your Name. and continued his international success with Weathering with You and Suzume.
