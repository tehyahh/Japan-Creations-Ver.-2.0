ABOUT THE CREATOR

Rumiko Takahashi

Rumiko Takahashi is one of Japan's most recognized manga artists. Her long-running career has produced several major works that have been adapted into anime, films, and other forms of media.

Her stories are especially known for memorable characters, energetic comedy, romance, supernatural elements, and a distinctive blend of different genres.

KNOWN FOR

Inuyasha

A time-traveling fantasy adventure following Kagome and the half-demon Inuyasha as they journey through feudal Japan.
