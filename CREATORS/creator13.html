ABOUT THE CREATOR

Tatsuki Fujimoto

Tatsuki Fujimoto is a Japanese manga artist whose work is recognized for combining action, horror, dark fantasy, absurd humor, and emotional drama.

His distinctive approach to storytelling has made him especially known for unpredictable narratives and characters who face unusual and often extreme circumstances.

KNOWN FOR
MANGA

Chainsaw Man

A dark action series following Denji, a young man who becomes the human host of the chainsaw devil and enters the dangerous world of devil hunters.

MANGA

Fire Punch

A dark fantasy story set in a frozen world, following Agni and his extraordinary regenerative ability.

ONE-SHOT

Look Back

An emotional story about two young artists whose shared passion for manga leads to a powerful friendship and personal journey.
