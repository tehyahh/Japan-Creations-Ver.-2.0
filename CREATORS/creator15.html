ABOUT THE CREATOR

Koyoharu Gotouge

Koyoharu Gotouge is a Japanese manga artist who created Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba. The series combines historical-inspired settings, supernatural elements, sword fighting, family bonds, and dramatic character journeys.

Demon Slayer became a major global franchise through its manga, anime adaptation, films, merchandise, and other media.
