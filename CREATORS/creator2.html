ABOUT THE CREATOR

Yūki Tabata

Yūki Tabata is a Japanese manga artist known for creating Black Clover. His storytelling combines fantasy battles, strong character relationships, humor, and themes of perseverance.

Black Clover began serialization in 2015 and became a major manga and anime franchise with a worldwide audience.
