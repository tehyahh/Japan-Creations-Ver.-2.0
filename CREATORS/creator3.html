ABOUT THE CREATOR

Hajime Isayama

Hajime Isayama is a Japanese manga artist whose work became internationally recognized through Attack on Titan. His storytelling is known for its layered mysteries, political themes, dramatic conflicts, and unexpected revelations.

Attack on Titan began serialization in 2009 and concluded in 2021, becoming one of the most influential manga and anime series of its generation.
