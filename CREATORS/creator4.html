ABOUT THE CREATOR

Hayao Miyazaki

Hayao Miyazaki is a Japanese animator, filmmaker, screenwriter, and co-founder of Studio Ghibli. His films are widely recognized for their detailed animation, imaginative storytelling, environmental themes, and memorable characters.

His body of work includes some of the most internationally acclaimed Japanese animated films, helping establish Japanese animation as a major global cinematic art form.
