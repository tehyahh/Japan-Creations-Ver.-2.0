ABOUT THE CREATOR

Masashi Kishimoto

Masashi Kishimoto is a Japanese manga artist best known for creating Naruto. His work combines action, character development, rivalry, friendship, and themes of perseverance.

Naruto began serialization in 1999 and became one of the most successful manga and anime franchises worldwide.
