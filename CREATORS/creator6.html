ABOUT THE CREATOR

Akira Toriyama

Akira Toriyama was a Japanese manga artist and character designer whose work had a major influence on manga, anime, games, and popular culture around the world.

His creations are known for energetic action, comedy, imaginative characters, and distinctive visual designs that became instantly recognizable across generations.
