ABOUT THE CREATOR

Gege Akutami

Gege Akutami is a Japanese manga artist best known for creating Jujutsu Kaisen. The series combines supernatural horror, intense action, dark humor, and complex character relationships.

Jujutsu Kaisen began serialization in 2018 and became one of the most prominent modern shōnen manga and anime franchises.
