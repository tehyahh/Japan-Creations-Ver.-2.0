ABOUT THE CREATOR

Tite Kubo

Tite Kubo is a Japanese manga artist best known for creating Bleach. His work is recognized for its memorable characters, supernatural battles, fashion-inspired designs, and distinctive artistic style.

Bleach began serialization in 2001 and became one of the major shōnen manga and anime franchises of the 2000s and beyond.
